## Overview

**Living Threat Repository** is a living cyber threat intelligence platform where real-world incidents are continuously mapped to **MITRE ATT&CK tactics and techniques** in a Navigator-style interface.

The platform helps defenders move beyond static reports by transforming threat intelligence into an interactive, explorable, and operational knowledge structure.

Users can filter intelligence, open reports, highlight technique chains, explore attack paths, and move into Hunt Pack views for deeper operational context.

---

## Why This Exists

Cybersecurity produces an enormous amount of intelligence every week:

- Threat advisories
- Incident reports
- Vendor research
- Malware analysis
- Threat actor investigations
- Security blogs and technical write-ups

But most of this intelligence becomes static once published.

Reports often disappear into PDFs, bookmarks, ticketing systems, and feeds. Security teams may read them once, extract a few indicators, and move on.

Yet the true purpose of threat intelligence is not simply to inform.

It is to improve defensive decisions.

**Living Threat Repository was built to make threat intelligence operational, searchable, visual, and continuously useful.**

---

## Core Idea

Instead of treating threat intelligence as static documents, the platform treats intelligence as a living structure that can be explored, mapped, and connected.

The repository maps real-world cyber incidents to attacker behavior, allowing security professionals to:

- Explore how attacks unfold across MITRE ATT&CK tactics
- Understand real incidents behind each technique
- Study attacker behavior instead of isolated indicators
- Bridge threat intelligence with security operations
- Support threat hunting and detection engineering
- Move from intelligence to operational action

---

## Key Capabilities

### ATT&CK Navigator-Style Mapping

Visualize real-world incidents mapped directly to MITRE ATT&CK tactics and techniques.

### Incident-to-Technique Correlation

Understand which techniques were used in actual attacks and how adversaries chained them together.

### Technique Chain Highlighting

Open a report and highlight the full ATT&CK technique chain associated with the incident.

### Attack Path View

Explore how an adversary progresses through an environment, from initial access to objectives.

### Hunt Pack Context

Move from intelligence into deeper hunting and detection-focused operational context.

### Living Intelligence Repository

Continuously connect incidents, techniques, malware, tools, threat actors, and defensive insights.
